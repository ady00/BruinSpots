Space For 5-8 people @ Powell Library

There are 6 booking option(s) available for the "Group Study Rooms" category and all zones. You can go back and change the filters.

Powell Group Study Rooms

⚠︎ JULY 25TH - JULY 30TH → RESERVATIONS WILL BE UNAVAILABLE DUE TO POTENTIAL BUILDING CLOSURE FOR MAINTENANCE. 
If the library building is open, the spaces are open for drop-in study on a first come, first serve basis. 

 

The Powell group study rooms are located in the reading room on the first floor of Powell Library.

All Powell group study rooms come equipped with:

  • Large table with chairs/couch (room seats 6-8)
  • White board 
  • Mobile screen which can be connected to via HDMI

VGA and HDMI adapters and cables, dry erase markers, and other items may be checked out at the CLICC Lab (Powell 145).

To book a group study room, select a time slot below, then scroll to the bottom of the page, set the length of your reservation using the drop down menu, then click "Submit Times". You will receive a confirmation email. If you don't see it within a few minutes of submitting your booking, check your spam or junk folder. 

If you need to cancel your booking, click on the link at the bottom of your confirmation email.

CLICC Group Study Rooms are available for drop-in study whenever the library is open but there is no active reservation for that room.

Terms & Conditions:

  • Current undergraduate and graduate students, faculty, and staff (all individuals currently eligible for CLICC services) may reserve up to 3 hours once every 24 hours up to 3 days in advance. Each individual can reserve up to 6 hours per week. UCLA Extension students are not elligible for room reservations.
  • Instructors and teaching assistants who wish to use a study room or pod for a small course or office hours should request an office hours or consultation space through the Reservations Portal.
  • Study rooms in YRL and Powell are now self-service. Reservation holders do not need to check in at a lending location before their reservation begins. QR codes placed at each group study room and collaboration pod can help verify reservation schedules. If patrons encounter any issues, they can always consult a CLICC consultant at the nearest lending location. 
  • If you have questions about the form or our policies, please submit an inquiry through the CLICC Resources & Services Portal. You can also check room availability by calling the CLICC lab at (310) 206-0271.
